What it is

Creative facilitation is the work that helps a stranger cross from watching to taking part. It decides whether a participatory work is welcoming, safe and actually creative. It is also the part of the job that is rarely named, rarely taught and rarely paid properly.

The Creative Facilitator Summit is a national gathering of the artists doing that work. Presented by Playable Streets with ArtPlay and Arts Centre Melbourne, it brings together facilitators, performers, producers, teaching artists and the organisations that hire them, from every state.

The two days

  • Day one at ArtPlay: practice in the room

    Workshops, live demonstrations and observed facilitation, plus the first public presentation of Glen Walton's doctoral research on the creative facilitator role and its four principles: openness, perception, authenticity and communication.

  • Day two at Arts Centre Melbourne: the field

    Keynote, panels and participant-led roundtables on the practical questions: rates and contracts, career pathways, training, safety, and how facilitators and the organisations that hire them find each other.

  • The lasting outcome: a national network

    The Summit launches the Creative Facilitators Network, a directory connecting facilitators with the organisations hiring them, by state and by skill area. It continues after the two days are over.

Who it's for

Artists who facilitate participation, whether or not you call yourself a facilitator. Performers, teaching artists, producers, front of house and program staff, museum and gallery educators, students and recent graduates, and anyone hiring people to do this work.

Places for independent artists are subsidised, with bursaries for artists travelling from regional Victoria and interstate. Registration opens in 2026.
